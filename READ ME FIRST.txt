===========================================================
  VENTUREBILL — BILLING SOFTWARE
  Complete Business Billing, GST & Inventory Platform
===========================================================

  Your invoices, clients, inventory, and accounting data
  stay safely on THIS computer.
  Offline-first. No mandatory cloud. Zero subscription fees.


-----------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 1 — EXTRACT THIS FOLDER FIRST
-----------------------------------------------------------

  If you are reading this INSIDE a ZIP file, extract it first.
  The software cannot run properly from inside an archive:

    1. Close this window.
    2. Right-click the VentureBill ZIP file.
    3. Choose "Extract All..." and extract to your desired folder.
    4. Open the extracted folder.

  Keep all files together. The launcher requires the "_system"
  folder located next to it.


-----------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 2 — RUN THE LAUNCHER FOR YOUR COMPUTER
-----------------------------------------------------------

  Double-click the launcher matching your operating system:

    Windows ..... VentureBill - WINDOWS.hta
    Mac ......... VentureBill - MAC.command
    Linux ....... VentureBill - LINUX.sh

  The launcher will check your environment, initialize the
  local service, and open VentureBill in your default browser
  or desktop view.


-----------------------------------------------------------
  SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
-----------------------------------------------------------

  - Node.js version 18 or newer
  - Windows 10/11, macOS 11+, or modern Linux distribution
  - Modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Safari)
